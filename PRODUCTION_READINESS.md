# Production readiness

Readiness combines live functionality, data/test integrity, security/privacy, accessibility, performance, recovery, commercial licensing, content correctness, source freshness, installer/update lifecycle, exact-artifact identity and GraphLock accounting.

Known blueprint-time external gates: UAT, manual accessibility, legal/license/privacy review, name/trademark clearance, signing credential and any provider-specific terms approval. They remain external; they cannot be simulated.
