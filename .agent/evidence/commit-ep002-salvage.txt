EP-002 salvage: recover the ASVAB subtest domain knowledge (REQ-004)

Assesses whether the stranded EP-000/EP-002 work is salvageable, and salvages
the part that is. Full reasoning in
.agent/evidence/EP-002/salvage-assessment.md.

RECOVERED from the unmerged branch feat/EP-001-desktop-foundation (0a51831),
which is 4 commits ahead of main and was never merged:

  Subtest now carries the official two-letter codes, learner-facing names, a
  case-insensitive from_code() parse, a canonical ALL list, and — the part that
  matters — is_afqt(), which encodes that the AFQT is composed of two verbal
  (WK, PC) and two mathematics (AR, MK) subtests. The other six contribute to
  line scores, never to the AFQT. The enum previously could not express this, so
  scoring logic treating all ten subtests as AFQT inputs would compute a wrong
  composite.

The branch's separate AsvabSubtest enum was deliberately NOT imported. Two enums
for one concept is the same duplicate-implementation problem as leaving a stub
beside its replacement.

REJECTED, with reasons recorded so neither is re-imported:

  - pack.rs verify_signature() is a string prefix match
    (starts_with("SIG_<pubkey>_")), not a signature check. It is worse than no
    check, because a reviewer would believe provenance had been verified.
  - learner_test.rs asserts the length of a vector the test itself built. It
    would pass if the enum had three variants. Its replacement asserts
    properties of the enum's own data and is proven non-vacuous by mutation:
    adding GS to the AFQT set, removing MK from it, and colliding two codes are
    all DETECTED.

Not adopted: vector-study/exam.rs is convergent with the EP-005 ExamSession,
which additionally enforces commit, lock and time-expiry.

EP-002 is NOT closed by this. Still outstanding: the hardcoded AdaptivePlan
stub (REQ-003), the dummy_signature stub (REQ-023), the recorded unit-tests
exit code of 1 on a NODE_DONE node, the three .log files that were never
committed, and the absent anti-gaming review.
