EP-002: remove the two domain stubs, implement real Ed25519 content-pack signing

Two stubs in crates/vector-domain implemented nothing while satisfying their
tests. Both are now gone.

1. plan::AdaptivePlan::generate returned a hardcoded ["drill_1", "drill_2"],
   ignoring the mastery state, the goal and the available time it was handed.
   DELETED rather than implemented, because the real planner already exists in
   vector_study::selection::generate_plan (EP-004) — it derives priority from
   due reviews, mastery distance and uncertainty, returns an empty plan when
   nothing needs work, and is covered by property tests over generated input.
   It cannot live in vector-domain because vector-study depends on it, so
   delegating would be circular. Two implementations of one concept, one real
   and one fake, is worse than one: a reader cannot tell which they have found.

2. ContentPack::sign returned the literal "dummy_signature", ignored the
   supplied key entirely, and force-set reviewed = true — three separate
   falsehoods in one method. REPLACED with real Ed25519 signing via
   ed25519-dalek 2 (BSD-3-Clause, within the release license policy).

   Design decisions worth recording:
   - The signature covers the content identity (name, version, content hash),
     NOT lifecycle state. If status were signed, every quarantine/rollback
     cycle would force a re-signature, training operators to re-sign casually.
   - Fields are length-prefixed. Without it the payload
     `name || u32le(version) || content_hash` collides: name="pack",
     version=1, hash="sha256:abc" produces identical bytes to name="pack\x01",
     version=0x73000000, hash="ha256:abc". A signature over the first would
     verify over the second. That collision is written out concretely in
     length_prefixing_prevents_a_real_payload_collision.
   - set_version clears the signature, because a new version is a new content
     identity and the old attestation no longer applies.
   - The type no longer claims review status. A signature proves who attested
     the content, never that a human reviewed it; review lives in the content
     QA ledger. The stub conflated the two, which would have let possession of
     a signing key stand in for a review.

Also removed: the domain copies of AnswerProof and QuestionItem, superseded by
the real implementations in vector_questions::ingestion (EP-007).

  - test_req_003_adaptive_plan asserted the drill list was non-empty, which the
