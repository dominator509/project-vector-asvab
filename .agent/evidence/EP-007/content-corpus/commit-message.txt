Paragraph Comprehension: passage store, 1,047 sourced items, and four defects

REQ-020, REQ-022, REQ-023, REQ-048, REQ-053, REQ-056.

Corpus
- `content ingest-pc` (tools/vector-tools) ingests Project Gutenberg works: one
  vault row per work, the title read from the file's own header, the URL built
  from the ebook number so a reviewer can re-download and get the same digest.
- 1,047 active PC items from 17 works, each citing the work it quotes. The
  application database now holds 2,000 WK + 3,668 EI + 1,047 PC = 6,715 items,
  with 8,715 citations and 26,860 review rows.

The passage the store was missing
- `migrations/005_content_passage.sql` adds `content_items.passage` and refuses,
  by trigger, a Paragraph Comprehension item without one. Before this a PC item
  could be stored and served with nothing to read: the question asks about a
  text and the text had nowhere to live.
- `ItemDto` and the interface carry it; `PracticeView` renders it before the
  question; the reader refuses a response that omits the field rather than
  treating absence as "no passage".

Defects found, each by a check that did not exist before
1. The passage builder split a paragraph into sentences and rejoined them with a
   space, so wherever the split was not at a space the item quoted text the work
   does not contain (`the cap of liberty.” An ancient` came back `night? ” “Where`).
   Passages are now cut from the paragraph by character span (`sentence_spans`),
   so they are the source's own text by construction.
2. Terminators inside initials and abbreviations split tokens in half (`R.R.`).
3. A list of illustrations became a passage, with index entries as its options.
4. `darwin-origin.txt` was cited as Project Gutenberg #2009 while the file is
   #1228: a false provenance claim that only a re-download could reveal. The 13
   affected items were deleted and re-ingested; 17/17 works now re-download to
   the bytes they are cited as.

Reachability
- The practice surface offered only the two generatable subtests, so the ingested
  corpus could not be practised. It now offers every subtest the device can
  serve, and never offers to generate one the factory cannot produce.

Tests
- `passages` 24, `pc_ingestion` 10, `content_lifecycle` 16, `content_commands` 22;
  new probes for corpus counts, ingestion reports and source provenance.
- Mutation proof: removing the source-containment check fails
  `storing_refuses_an_item_whose_passage_is_not_in_the_source`.
- Four E2E tests had been failing since the corpus replaced the sample literals,
  asserting option text from `sample.ts`. Proven pre-existing by running the same
  specs at 1ae9dce in a worktree, then fixed with a shared stub of the Tauri
  bridge and by reading the correct option and item id from the page.
