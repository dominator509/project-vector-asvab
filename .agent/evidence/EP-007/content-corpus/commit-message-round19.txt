content: the rest of NEETS, eight more works, and a run that could be ended by one thin module (REQ-022, REQ-023, REQ-056)

The Electronics Information bank goes from 414 to 1,565 questions and the corpus from 3,809 to
5,598, and two defects fell out of doing it: a single thin module could end a twenty-four-module
ingestion after it had already stored 1,152 items, and the question check had made ingestion
quadratic.

## What it adds

**The rest of NEETS.** The Electronics Information bank was built from ten of the Navy
Electricity and Electronics Training Series' twenty-four modules, because ten were what an
earlier round happened to download. The collection is one Internet Archive item with
twenty-four text files; `scripts/probes/fetch-archive-text.py` gained `--list` (and a file-name
form) to show that, the other fourteen modules are fetched, and ingesting all twenty-four
produces **1,565 questions** where ten produced 414.

**Eight more Paragraph Comprehension works.** The manifest holds seventeen Project Gutenberg
works and now holds twenty-five: Agricola's *De Re Metallica*, Whewell's *History of the
Inductive Sciences*, Candolle's *Origin of Cultivated Plants*, Thorndike's *A History of Magic
and Experimental Science*, two volumes of Kirby and Spence's *Introduction to Entomology*, and
two of Pliny's *Natural History*. They add **626 questions** (1,050 -> 1,676), all of them
passages quoted verbatim from the work they cite.

`scripts/probes/fetch-gutenberg.py` fetches them in the one URL form the provenance check
re-derives, and it reads each file's own Gutenberg header before writing it: a download whose
`[eBook #...]` disagrees with the number it was asked for is refused. That check exists because
round 18 found ten items citing #2009 for a file that is #1228 -- a citation that looked
authoritative and could not be re-checked.

**The relation these manuals name instead of stating.** `The purpose of the piston skirt is to
keep the piston from rocking in the cylinder` puts the subject *after* the phrase, so reading
what precedes the verb names `purpose`. `scripts/probes/description-shapes.py` counted the shape
before it was read -- nineteen sentences in TM 9-8000 -- and reading `function of X is to Y` and
`purpose of X is to Y` takes the two automotive manuals from 78 descriptions to 97 and the Auto
Information bank from 59 to **70 questions**: `hair spring`, `engine lubrication`, `piston
skirt`, `air-over-hydraulic suspension system`.

The shape needs one guard the verb-before shapes do not: a subject that opens with a gerund is
an action, not a thing. `The purpose of burning fuel in the priming cup is to thoroughly heat
the vaporizing chamber` describes burning fuel, and reading the noun phrase out of it named
`burning fuel`, then `idle system` from `The purpose of shutting off the idle system with the
engine is to help eliminate engine dieseling`. Both are refused, and both refusals are asserted
with the sentences verbatim from the sources.

`as` joins the words that mean a name is a clause, because `The differential, as explained in
the preceding paragraph, is to provide for differences in speed` named `as explained`.

## The defect: a thin module ended the run

Ingesting the twenty-four modules in one command **failed**. The pipeline refuses a module that
builds no items -- correctly, because a caller who asked for one module and got nothing must be
told -- and the loop propagated that refusal with `?`. Module 24's glossary holds eight entries
and none survives the filters, so the run aborted on the last module **after storing 1,152 items
from the other twenty-three**, and never wrote its report: the work was done and the evidence
for it was lost.

The Paragraph Comprehension path learned this in round 15 ("a work that parses badly cannot take
the rest down with it"), and this loop carried the same promise in its own doc comment while
breaking it. A module that contributes nothing is now recorded as `skipped` with its reason and
the run continues; a run in which *no* module produced an item still fails, which is the guard
that keeps a broken scan from reporting a successful empty ingestion. Both behaviours have
tests, and both mutations are caught.

## The defect: ingestion had become quadratic

Round 18's question identity runs once per *built candidate*, and a builder produces far more
candidates than items -- the Electronics path builds about 10,000 candidates per run to find a
few hundred questions. Without an index that predicate scanned `content_items` every time, and
ingesting twenty-four modules had not finished after ten minutes where the same work without the
check took about three. `migrations/008_content_question_index.sql` adds
`(subtest, LOWER(TRIM(stem)))`: the expression rather than `stem`, because the comparison is
case- and space-insensitive by design, and a plain index would not be used. The application
embeds it with the other seven migrations, so a user's corpus gets it too.

## A third fix, in the rebuild script

`scripts/rebuild-corpus.py` takes `--only <subtests>` and now holds the source order itself:
sorted module paths, the manifest for the works. The order is not cosmetic -- each module's seed
is the run's seed plus its index, so the same twenty-four modules came out as 1,566 questions
under one shell's glob order and 1,508 under another's.

## Sources surveyed and refused

Ten Army ordnance maintenance manuals were downloaded and measured for the Auto Information
shape and refused: TM 9-1765B, TM 9-1828A, TM 9-1829A, TM 9-1729A, TM 9-1750, TM 9-1765A,
TM 9-1727C, TM 9-1868, TM 9-1710C and TM 9-1803B. Between them they hold **two** sentences of
that shape, because they are repair procedures (`Remove the four bolts`, `Install the gasket`)
and the relation `X is used to Y` belongs to the principles manuals. *Machinery's Reference*
booklets 1 and 7 hold three descriptions each. The ERIC microfiche manuals refused in round 18
stay refused.

`fetch-archive-text.py --list`, `fetch-gutenberg.py`, `rebuild-corpus.py --only`, and the
mutation harness now proves 20 rules instead of 18.
