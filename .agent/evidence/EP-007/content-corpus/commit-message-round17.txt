Shop Information, and an OCR check that was doing nothing

REQ-022, REQ-023, REQ-048, REQ-056.

Shop Information
- `crates/vector-questions/src/purposes.rs` reads a tool manual describing what each tool
  is for and forms an item from it: the purpose clause is the manual's own words, the
  question is built around it, and the distractors are tools the same manual describes
  doing something else.
- `content ingest-tools` wires it through the pipeline, the vault and the CLI. Manuals are
  Internet Archive scans, so a work is named `<archive-id>:<title>=<path>`; the id makes
  the recorded URL resolvable.
- 31 items from *Tools and Their Uses* (NAVEDTRA rate training manual, 1971) and *TM 11-453
  Shop Work* (US Army Signal Corps, 1942).

The OCR check, which was doing nothing and then doing harm
- On the factual and shop paths it was handed whole sentences and whole options, and it
  answers a question about a word, so it could not fail. Found by a test that insisted a
  misread tool must not reach an option: `inuide micrometer` had.
- Fixed to run word by word, it immediately refused 226 of 301 General Science items, every
  refusal an ordinary English word: `produced`, `caused`, `applied`, `began`, `countries`,
  `today`. Those are one edit from their own base forms, because the inflection helper does
  not know that `-ed` follows a silent `e`.
- It now runs only where its precision was measured: NEETS glossary terms (EI), and every
  word of a tool-name option (SI), where a tool is one to three common nouns and an unknown
  word is genuinely suspect. General Science carries no dictionary check, and its corpus
  returns from 209 items to 301.
- `scripts/probes/check-options.py` reads the stored corpus -- not the ingester's report --
  and finds 0 options containing scan damage across 7,047 items. It reports two Word
  Knowledge options containing a rare word (`unendowed`, `becharm`) as a separate category:
  that is the corpus's oldest open gap, and it is not scan damage.

Refused sources
- Machinery's Reference Series booklets 3, 11 and 12 were downloaded, measured and refused.
  Their scans mangle tool names into `MaWng Oonoave Forming`, `Drill Jiers Drill` and
  `punch does not`; shipping them would have put visibly broken options in front of learners
  to make a count look better.
- Auto Information still has no items: the Navy automotive manual's review questions refer to
  figures that are not in the item, and the Project Gutenberg automotive texts are
  essayistic.
