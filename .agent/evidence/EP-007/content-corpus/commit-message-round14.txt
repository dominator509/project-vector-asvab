Mechanical Comprehension and General Science: 301 sourced items and eight proofs

REQ-020, REQ-022, REQ-023, REQ-048, REQ-056.

Mechanical Comprehension, generated rather than quoted
- Eight templates in `crates/vector-questions/src/factory.rs`: lever effort, gear
  ratio, block and tackle, wheel and axle, piston pressure, hydraulic jack,
  inclined plane, and mechanical advantage. Each answer is arithmetic over the
  machine's own geometry, so each carries an executable proof of the same kind an
  Arithmetic Reasoning item carries, and `verify` recomputes it.
- Every generation property in `tests/factory.rs` now runs over MC as well as AR
  and MK: positive integer options, four distinct options, a named misconception per
  distractor, reproduction from a seed, and all declared templates exercised.
- The practice surface offers the subtest, because the factory can serve it.

General Science from a question-and-answer work
- `crates/vector-questions/src/facts.rs` reads a public-domain science book's own
  questions and answers. The question is the stem, the answer's first sentence is
  the correct option quoted verbatim, and the distractors are the same book's
  answers to other questions -- each a true statement about something else, which is
  what makes the item turn on knowledge rather than on recognising an answer's shape.
- `verify` re-derives every fact: the source must give this answer to this question,
  and every distractor must answer a different question in the same work.
- The OCR check the other ingested paths use is applied to the question and every
  option, because the corpus mixes modern reprints with scanned manuals.
- Markup and headings are refused as answers. `~WHERE RUBBER COMES FROM~ The first
  class, or wild rubbers ...` and an `[Illustration: ...]` caption both reached items
  as options before that filter existed.

Honest gaps, with the reconnaissance recorded so it is not repeated
- Shop Information: `Tools and Their Uses` (NAVEDTRA, 1971) holds ~30 sentences of
  the form `X is used to <purpose>`; not yet mined.
- Auto Information: the Navy automotive manual's review questions refer to figures
  and are unusable without them, and the Project Gutenberg automotive texts are
  essayistic (0-2 structured statements per work). No source found yet.
- ERIC vocational curricula were deliberately not used: their copyright status is
  not clearly federal, and the corpus admits only works whose terms are established.
- Definition mining was measured and abandoned: a sentence-initial `<term> is a
  <class> that ...` rule yields 2-5 usable definitions per manual, and corroborating
  against Webster's entry rejected good pairs while accepting bad ones. The probes
  that measured this are committed.
- The 1910s science book is a period source, and nothing in the pipeline can
  establish that a 1913 explanation is current. Stated in the module documentation.
