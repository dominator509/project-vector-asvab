what blocks them. The fix is not another pattern -- a reason may now name its blocker explicitly as
`blocked-by: <kind>:<name>`, a declaration that survives its prose being rewritten. Second, the
change-invalidation graph pulled `dependency-audit`, `build` and everything downstream of the
artifact into the rerun list from the lockfile change alone, which is why the sweep that followed
the upgrade is the one whose evidence counts.
