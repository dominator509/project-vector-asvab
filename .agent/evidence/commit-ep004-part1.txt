EP-004 (part 1): FSRS scheduling and adaptive study planning

Implements the learning-mechanics half of EP-004 on top of the EP-003
persistence layer.

REQ-043 (FSRS-compatible review engine):
- fsrs.rs implements the FSRS-4.5 memory model: power forgetting curve
  R(t) = (1 + t/(9S))^-1, its inverse for retention-targeted intervals,
  initial stability/difficulty curves, difficulty mean reversion, stability
  growth on success, and stability collapse on lapse.
- Parameters are a persisted 17-weight structure with validation, so a
  schedule is reproducible from stored state (SPEC-001) and a corrupt
  parameter set is refused rather than silently producing nonsense.

REQ-011 (readiness) and REQ-003 (adaptive plan):
- selection.rs replaces the previous placeholder planner that returned
  hard-coded "drill_1"/"drill_2" names. Priority now derives from real
  inputs: due reviews, mastery distance from goal, and estimate uncertainty.
- Readiness is emitted as a band that widens with uncertainty and subtest
  disagreement. official_score_claim is always false (ADR-010,
  SCORING_AND_READINESS.md): no precise predicted score before a validation
  cohort exists.
- A learner at goal with nothing due receives an EMPTY plan, so the planner
  cannot pad the schedule with busy-work.

Evidence: 25 tests (13 FSRS + 12 selection). 8/8 meaningful mutations detected,
including lapse handling, forgetting-curve inversion, stability growth,
due-review weighting, time-budget enforcement, and the readiness-band width.
